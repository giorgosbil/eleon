Eleon
Self-hosted setup

Let’s get Eleon running.

Keep customer emails, supplier replies and team messages in one case. Start here to install Eleon on your computer or server, then connect your first mailbox.

  • About 30–40 minutes
  • Runs on your own computer or server
  • Free and open source

Steps

    Change settings

    For an installation that already runs: load its .env, change what you need, download it again and apply it (bottom of the page).

    What you need

    Choose your computer and who will use it (above), then check these.

    • A computer that stays on: Windows 10 or 11 (Pro, Enterprise or Education)Ubuntu 22.04 or newermacOS 13 or newer, 8 GB memory, 50 GB free disk, internet.
    • Your mail: Microsoft 365 / Outlook, or Google Workspace. You (or your IT person) must be able to register an app in its admin portal. Step 4 shows how.
    • The server's address on your network: its IP address (for example 192.168.1.20) or a name (for example cases.office.lan).
    • No licence key. The full application is open source under AGPL-3.0-only. Optional AI needs a provider or a compatible local endpoint.

    "Cannot find path" / "Start.ps1 is not recognized" / "No such file or directory"

    Stuck?
    
          
    "Not secure" or "Your connection is not private"
    
          
    The certificate is not installed on that computer, or it went into the wrong store. On Windows it must be Local Machine → Trusted Root Certification Authorities. Close every browser window and open the address again.
    
          
    It opens on the server but not from other computers
    
          
    The firewall blocks the port: run the second line of the command above on the server. Check that the address in step 3 is the server's network address, not localhost.
